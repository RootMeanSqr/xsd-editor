## Why this exists

The Project is licensed under [Apache-2.0](LICENSE), and §5 of that licence already makes inbound
contributions Apache-2.0 by default. This agreement asks for one thing beyond that: the right to
**sublicense** (§2 above).

That right is what would let the Project be relicensed or offered under different terms in the
future — a commercial edition, or a change of licence — without tracking down every past
contributor for permission. That is not a plan, and the licensing decision record
([`docs/decisions/0001-licensing.md`](docs/decisions/0001-licensing.md)) records Apache-2.0 as the
intended end state. The agreement exists because the option is cheap to keep now and effectively
impossible to add later.

You keep every right you had. Nothing here stops you using your own contribution however you like,
including in your own projects under any licence.
